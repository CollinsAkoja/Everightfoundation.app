About Everight

Dedicated to Health & Community Wellness

We believe every person deserves access to quality healthcare. Our foundation works tirelessly to bridge the gap between underserved communities and essential medical services.

👁️ Our Vision

A Nigeria where healthcare is accessible, affordable, and equitable for all citizens regardless of background.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver life-changing health interventions through community outreach, preventive education, and sustainable partnerships.

⭐ Core Values

Compassion, Integrity, Community, Excellence, and Sustainability guide every intervention we deliver.

Our Programs

Healthcare Services & Campaigns

Comprehensive health programs designed to uplift communities and save lives.

Cancer Awareness

Early detection screenings and education programs to reduce cancer mortality rates.

Medical Outreach

Free medical camps and mobile clinics serving remote communities.

Food Programs

Nutritional support and food security initiatives for vulnerable families.

Maternal Care

Prenatal support, safe delivery programs, and postnatal care for mothers.

WASH Campaigns

Clean water, sanitation, and hygiene education for healthier communities.

Community Screening

Free health checkups, diabetes testing, and blood pressure monitoring.
